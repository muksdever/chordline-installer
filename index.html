Chordline

Our own Discord mod. Plugins, badges and extras, made for the crew.

Download for Windows

ChordlineInstaller.exe · always the latest version

How to install

  1. 1
    Download and open the installerRun ChordlineInstaller.exe from your Downloads.
  2. 2
    If Windows warns you, click "More info" → "Run anyway"The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it yet.
  3. 3
    Pick your Discord and click InstallWorks with Discord, Discord PTB and Discord Canary. Discord restarts with Chordline loaded.

What you get

  • 100+ pluginsEverything from Vencord, plus your own.
  • Make your own pluginsWrite, import and share plugins right in Discord.
  • Chordline badgesExclusive badges you can hide or show like real ones.
  • Auto updatesNew versions arrive through the built-in updater.
